A real cross-backend comparison on real data: the passive cart-pole scenario captured on PhysX and on Newton/MJWarp, generated 2026-07-12 on an NVIDIA (Blackwell) consumer GPU by isaaclab_contrib.parity and vendored into this repository as a fixture. No GPU and no Isaac Lab are needed to run it: IVF reads the trajectory bundles with numpy. This manifest is written to demonstrate the honest outcome rather than a flattering one. Two backends integrating the same passive articulation are not expected to agree to a tight tolerance over 480 steps, and the interesting question is not "do they match" but "does IVF say something defensible about how they differ, and does it refuse to overclaim where the evidence does not support a claim". Note in particular the solver-settings control below. These bundles record no solver configuration at all, so IVF reports that control as unverifiable rather than matched. That is a real gap in the released evidence, not a formality.
